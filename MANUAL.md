## 1. Story & Prologue
The year is 1991. The rain-slicked streets of Chinatown are held in the iron grip of the **Shadow Dragon Clan Syndicate**, led by the imperial martial artist **Grandmaster Kuroda** and his lieutenants: the treacherous **Master Shen** and the demonic brute **Iron Fang Oni**.

From underground ninja dojos to moving rooftop bullet trains, towering construction cranes, and the legendary 100-floor Pagoda, the syndicate has unleashed an army of street thugs, shadow ninjas, blade ronin, tengu kickboxers, kanabo club giants, and forbidden reanimated zombies.

Take to the streets or ascend the pagoda to dismantle the syndicate floor by floor!

## 5. The Legendary Dragon Knuckle (Mega Punch)

The Captain Falcon Homage! Inspired by the legendary *"FALCON PUNCH"*, this is the single most destructive move in the game:
1. **The Charge**: Pull back into an immovable crane stance with **Super Armor**. Swirling golden and cyan Ki particles vortex inward into your clenched fist with an 8-bit rising audio crescendo: *"DRAAAAGOON..."*
2. **The Rocket Thrust**: Rocket forward at extreme speed (`vx = 8.4`). An enormous **Fiery Pixel Dragon Head** erupts from your knuckles with teeth, horns, and glowing cyan eyes, blasting through crowds for **65 massive damage** and screen-clearing launch: *"...KNUCKLE!!!!"*
3. **Trigger Methods**:
   - Dedicated key: <kbd>H</kbd> or <kbd>U</kbd>
   - Hold Punch (<kbd>J</kbd>) for 0.35s and release
   - Dash + Punch (<kbd>J</kbd>)
   - Forward + Special (<kbd>L</kbd>)
   - Gamepad Right Trigger (<kbd>RT / R2</kbd>)
   - Touch <kbd>MEGA</kbd> button

## 9. 2P Co-Op AI Companion System

Play alongside a smart, loyal martial arts fighting partner!
* **Bro Protection**: Peels off flankers and protects your blind spots.
* **Pincer Co-Op Positioning**: Always flanks targets from the opposite side of the human player and attacks from a staggered depth lane so you never overlap or block each other's attacks.
* **Autonomous Attack Cadence**: Attacks with human-like rhythm and reaction pacing rather than firing instantaneously on frame 0.
* **Anti-Mirror Protocol**: Actively detects your current moves and executes complementary, contrasting martial arts techniques (e.g. if you punch, they unleash kicks, sweeps, or launchers; they will never double-cast Mega Punch or Special at the same time).
* **Character Archetypes**:
  - **Lindin (Agile Viper)**: Specializes in acrobatic Flash Kicks, sweeping heel drops, and rapid dagger strikes.
  - **Smoro (Titan Bull)**: Specializes in heavy gut hooks, ground shockwaves, and lead pipe smashes.
  - **Smellu (Balanced Dragon)**: Specializes in Shoryu uppercuts, 5-hit punch rushes, and One-Inch Ki blasts.
* **Rear Perimeter Lookout**: When in idle formation, the companion faces outward to watch your rear guard rather than mirroring your exact facing.
* **Revival Mechanic**: If knocked out, enters a 6-second recovery state with a live HUD timer and leaps back into the fight with full health!
* **Tactics (Configurable in Settings)**:
  - `BALANCED`: Smart positioning, flanking, combo chains into specials.
  - `AGGRESSIVE`: Relentless frontal assault, dive kicks, frequent Mega Punches.
  - `PROTECTOR`: Bodyguard mode, sticks close to player, intercepts enemies targeting player.
* **Hotkey**: Press <kbd>T</kbd> or <kbd>C</kbd> at any time to summon or dismiss.

## 10. Enemy Bestiary & Boss Strategies

* **Street Thugs**: Pack rushers who flank and strike with brawler jabs. Disarm them for bonus weapons!
* **Shadow Ninjas**: Agile shinobi with quick slash attacks and leap kicks. Counter with Low Sweeps!
* **Heavy Bruisers**: High-poise tanks that wind up heavy charging haymakers. Use Rising Dragon or Dragon Knuckle!
* **Blade Ronin**: Armored samurai in straw kasa hats delivering long-reach dash slashes. Jump or backflip over slashes!
* **Tengu Kickers**: Acrobats in red Tengu masks delivering flying dive kicks. Intercept with Air Hammer Drop!
* **Shadow Assassins**: Fast kunoichi in violet cowls with reverse-grip daggers. Keep them pinned with Gun-Fu!
* **Club Giants**: 7-foot ogres with spiked iron kanabo clubs. Deliver devastating ground-shattering smashes with super-armor.
* **Rotten Zombies**: Decaying walking corpses! Double claw swipes and leaping acid saliva bites. Explode into massive green gore showers!
* **The 3 Grandmasters**:
  - **Master Shen**: Agile grandmaster with spinning aerial kicks.
  - **Iron Fang Oni**: Demonic brute with shockwave ground stomps.
  - **Grandmaster Kuroda**: Shadow Dragon Emperor with imperial Ki waves.

## 11. Dynamic Speed Scaling, Secrets & Pro Tips

### Enemy Speed Scaling Formula
Enemies become progressively faster, sharper, and more aggressive the higher your score climbs!
$$\text{Speed Multiplier} = 1.0 + \left(\frac{\text{Score}}{100}\right) \times 0.005$$
* **0 PTS**: 1.00x Base Speed (+0%)
* **1,000 PTS**: 1.05x Speed (+5%)
* **5,000 PTS**: 1.25x Speed (+25%)
* **10,000 PTS**: 1.50x Speed (+50%)
* **20,000 PTS**: 2.00x Speed (+100% / Double Speed!)

### Pro Combat Tips
1. **Arterial Green Gore**: Striking enemies spurts green gore droplets that pool onto the wet pavement and reflect neon lighting.
2. **Armed Regular Enemies**: Regular enemies have a ~32% chance to spawn wielding weapons and guarantee a **100% weapon drop** upon defeat.
3. **CRT Scanlines**: Toggle CRT scanlines and curved phosphor glow via the footer button.
4. **8-Bit Soundtrack Jukebox**: Preview and jam out to authentic overdriven 8-bit rock tracks in the Settings menu!
