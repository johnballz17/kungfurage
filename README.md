## 👥 AI Companion (2P Co-Op Fighting Partner)

Play with an intelligent, loyal martial arts partner who fights alongside you in real-time!
- **Bro Protection**: Actively detects when enemies swarm or attack the player and charges in to peel them off.
- **Pincer Boss Flanks**: Automatically positions on the opposite side of Bosses (Master Shen, Iron Fang Oni, Kuroda) to execute deadly cross-arena pincher combos.
- **Full Martial Arts Arsenal**: Chains rapid punches, kicks, **360° Tornado Spin Kicks**, **Front Flips / Backflips**, and unleashes **Dragon Specials** and the **DRAGON KNUCKLE** when facing high-threat foes!
- **Revive Mechanic**: If knocked out, enters a 6-second recovery state and leaps back into the fight with full health. You are never left permanently alone!
- **Instant Hotkey**: Press <kbd>T</kbd> or <kbd>C</kbd> anywhere (Character Select or during gameplay) to instantly summon or dismiss your companion.
- **Configurable via Settings Menu**:
  - **Status**: Toggle ON / OFF with live audio confirmation.
  - **Partner Character**: `AUTO MATCHUP` (balanced complementary pair), `LINDIN` (Agile Viper), `SMELLU` (Balanced Dragon), or `SMORO` (Heavy Iron Bull).
  - **Combat Tactics**:
    - `BALANCED`: Smart positioning, flanking bosses, combo chains into specials.
    - `AGGRESSIVE`: Relentless frontal assault, dive kicks, frequent Mega Punches.
    - `PROTECTOR`: Bodyguard mode, sticks close to player, intercepts enemies targeting player.

## 🥋 Game Features & Mechanics

1. **DRAGON KNUCKLE — The Legendary "Falcon Punch" Homage**:
   - **The Charge**: Pull back into an unstoppable crane stance with **Super Armor** as fiery Ki particles swirl inward with an 8-bit rising crescendo sound (*"DRAAAAGOON..."*).
   - **The Thrust**: Rocket forward with massive speed (`vx = 8.4`) as an enormous **Pixel-Art Flaming Dragon Head** bursts from your fist with teeth, horns, and glowing cyan eyes!
   - **The Impact**: 65 damage, 9-frame hit-stop, violent screen shake, explosive flame bursts, and an earth-shattering boom (*"...KNUCKLE!!!!"*). Blasts through multiple enemies and smashes crates to splinters!
   - **Multiple Ways to Trigger**:
     - Dedicated key: <kbd>H</kbd>, <kbd>U</kbd>, or <kbd>B</kbd>
     - **Hold Punch** (<kbd>J</kbd>/<kbd>Z</kbd>) for 0.35s and release
     - **Dash + Punch** or **Forward + Special** (<kbd>L</kbd>)
     - Gamepad: **RT / R2** (Right Trigger)
     - Touchscreen: Glowing pulsating **MEGA** button
   - **Hero-Specific Callouts**:
     - **Smellu**: *"DRAAAAGOON... KNUCKLE!!!"* (Blazing Golden Dragon)
     - **Lindin**: *"VIIIPEEER... STRIKE!!!"* (Cyan/Magenta Viper/Phoenix)
     - **Smoro**: *"TITAAAN... SMASH!!!"* (Crimson/Gold Bull Demon)
2. **Colossal 16,000px Marathon Stages, 20 Encounter Zones & Reinforcement Ambushes**:
   - Stages are a colossal **16,000 pixels long** (over 6.6× standard arcade levels) featuring **20 distinct encounter zones** per stage:
     - Opening street / rooftop skirmishes
     - Weapon crate caches & supply drops
     - Mid-stage ambush gauntlets
     - **Dynamic Reinforcement Waves**: When enemies drop to 2 or fewer, a second wave of ambushers sprints in with an arcade `"REINFORCEMENTS!"` banner!
     - Mid-Stage Mini-Boss Arenas in Zones 5, 10, and 15
     - Pre-boss elite guard corridors
     - Grand Boss Arenas at Zone 20 with cinematic villain entrances!
   - Real-time **Zone HUD tracker** (`ZONE 1 / 20` through `ZONE 20 / 20`) with an integrated **Mini Distance Progress Bar** showing player progress across the entire 16,000px expanse.
   - Deeper walking lane depth (`Y: 152px - 264px`) allows full vertical dodging and maneuvering.
   - Dynamic vertical camera tracking (`camY`) and strict jump ceiling clamping ensure fighters never jump off-screen.
2. **8-Bit Retro Arcade Boss Laugh**:
   - Synthesized with real-time staccato square-wave arpeggio pulses ("HA - HA - HA - HA - HAA!").
   - Distinct vocal pitch profiles for each villain:
     - **Master Shen**: Sharp, sinister grandmaster cackle.
     - **Iron Fang Oni**: Deep, guttural demonic rumble laugh.
     - **Grandmaster Kuroda**: Layered imperial Shadow Dragon laugh.
   - Triggers on boss intro entrances, enrage state transitions, and when landing heavy knockdowns on the player! Bosses visually laugh with animated heads, bobbing shoulders, and comic `"MWAHAHAHA!"` speech bubbles.
3. **8 Distinct Enemy Archetypes**:
   - **Street Thugs**: Pack rushers who flank and strike with brawler combos.
   - **Shadow Ninjas**: Agile leaping shinobi with quick slash attacks and circular flanks.
   - **Heavy Bruisers**: High-poise tanks that wind up heavy charging haymakers.
   - **Blade Ronin**: Armored katana masters wearing straw kasa hats and samurai haori. Execute long-reach Iaijutsu dash slashes with gleaming cyan sword arcs.
   - **Tengu Acrobatic Kickboxers**: Fast martial artists in red Tengu masks who leap into the air for flying dive kicks and rapid roundhouse flurries.
   - **Shadow Kunoichi Assassins**: Fast, agile killers in violet cowls with pink sashes wielding twin reverse-grip daggers. Zig-zag flanking and criss-cross flurry slashes.
   - **Tetsubo Club Giants**: Towering 7-foot ogres in tiger-skin pelts wielding massive spiked iron kanabo clubs. Deliver devastating ground-shattering overhead smashes with screen shake and super-armor.
   - **Undead Shambling Zombies (NEW!)**: Decaying rotten-green walking corpses with exposed ribs and hollow glowing yellow eyes! They shamble and lurch unpredictably, emit eerie synthesized undead groans, and unleash **Double Claw Swipes** and terrifying **Leaping Bite Chomps** with dripping green acid saliva! When destroyed, they explode into a massive shower of rotting green gore and giblet chunks!
4. **Dynamic Score-Based Enemy Speed Scaling (+0.5% per 100 Score)**:
   - Enemies become progressively faster, sharper, and more aggressive the higher your score climbs!
   - **Formula**: `Speed Multiplier = 1.0 + (Score / 100) * 0.005` (+0.5% speed per 100 points):
     - **0 Score**: 1.00x Base Speed (+0%)
     - **1,000 Score**: 1.05x Speed (+5%)
     - **5,000 Score**: 1.25x Speed (+25%)
     - **10,000 Score**: 1.50x Speed (+50%)
     - **20,000 Score**: 2.00x Speed (+100% / Double Speed!)
   - **Comprehensive Impact on Enemy Combat**:
     - **Movement & Flanking Velocity**: Enemies sprint, stalk, and close distances faster.
     - **Attack Strike Frequency**: Attack cooldowns shrink inversely with score so enemies unleash combos with shorter delays between attacks.
     - **Animation Execution Speed**: Attack animations and flying slashes land quicker.
     - **Boss Enrage Synergy**: Stacks multiplicatively with Boss Enrage boosts (up to 2.7x speed for enraged bosses at high score!).
     - **Live HUD Speed Indicator**: Real-time `ENEMY SPD +X%` readout under the score counter on the HUD.
5. **Visceral Green Gore & Blood Splatter System**:
   - Striking enemies unleashes high-velocity arterial sprays of vivid toxic green blood (`#39ff14`, `#00ff44`, `#1b7a2b`).
   - Gore particles have realistic arc physics and gravity: droplets spurt outward in the attack direction and stick to the rainy pavement, forming lingering reflective floor gore puddles!
   - Heavy hits, knockdowns, weapon strikes, and zombie kills tear off tumbling meaty green giblet chunks with internal toxic cores!
