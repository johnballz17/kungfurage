CHAPTER 1: THE DRAGON CRIME WAVE

SHADOWS OVER CHINATOWN

The year is 1991. The rain-slicked streets of Chinatown are held in the iron grip of the Shadow Dragon Clan Syndicate, led by the enigmatic martial artist Grandmaster Kuroda and his ruthless lieutenants: the treacherous Master Shen and the demonic brute Iron Fang Oni.

From underground ninja dojos to high-speed trains, soaring construction cranes, and ancient mountain pagodas, the syndicate has unleashed an army of street thugs, ninja assassins, armored blade ronin, tengu kickboxers, kanabo-wielding giants, and forbidden reanimated zombies!

YOUR MISSION: Take to the neon streets or scale the towering pagoda to dismantle the syndicate floor by floor. Master martial arts combos, wield deadly black market weapons, unleash the legendary Dragon Knuckle, and reclaim the city!

CHAPTER 5: THE ULTIMATE TECHNIQUE

DRAGON KNUCKLE (MEGA PUNCH)

THE CAPTAIN FALCON HOMAGE: Inspired by the legendary "FALCON PUNCH", the Dragon Knuckle is the single most destructive move in Carpe's Kung Fu Rage!

PHASE 1: THE CHARGE

Pull back into an immovable crane stance granted with Super Armor. Golden and cyan Ki particles vortex inward into your clenched fist with an 8-bit rising audio crescendo: "DRAAAAGOON..."

PHASE 2: ROCKET THRUST & IMPACT

Rocket forward at extreme velocity (`vx = 8.4`). An enormous Fiery Dragon Head erupts from your knuckles with teeth, horns, and cyan eyes, blasting through crowds for 65 massive damage and screen-clearing launch: "...KNUCKLE!!!!"

WAYS TO TRIGGER: Press H or U • Hold Punch (J) for 0.35s and release • Dash + Punch • Forward + Special (L) • Gamepad RT / R2 • Touch MEGA button!

CHAPTER 9: CO-OP FIGHTING PARTNER

2P AI COMPANION SYSTEM

Fight alongside an intelligent martial arts partner who covers your back, peels off flankers, and coordinates pincer combos against bosses!

COMBAT TACTICS & ANTI-MIRRORING

  • PINCER FLANKS: Always encircles targets from the opposite side of the player on a staggered depth lane.
  • ANTI-MIRROR PROTOCOL: Deliberately chooses contrasting attacks (e.g. kicks/launchers when you punch) to prevent twin move cloning.
  • REAR PERIMETER: Guards outward defensively when in formation to watch your blind spots.
  • BALANCED: Smart positioning, flanking bosses, combo chains into specials.
  • AGGRESSIVE: Relentless frontal assault, dive kicks, frequent Mega Punches.
  • PROTECTOR: Bodyguard mode, sticks close to player, intercepts enemies targeting player.

REVIVAL MECHANIC

If your partner is knocked out, they enter a 6-second recovery state with a live HUD timer. Once the timer expires, they leap back into combat with full health. You are never left permanently alone!

SUMMON HOTKEY: Press T or C at any time to summon or dismiss your partner!

Enemies become progressively faster and sharper the higher your score climbs!
